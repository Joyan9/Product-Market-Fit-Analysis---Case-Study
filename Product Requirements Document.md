# Product Requirements Document: AirPure Air Purifier

## Product Positioning
**Year-round protection system engineered for India's pollution patterns**
- Target: NCR metro families (Delhi, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, Noida, Gurugram)
- Core insight: 81% of winter days + 41% of summer days require filtration (not seasonal product)

---

## Must-Have Features

### Core Filtration
| Feature | Justification | BOM Cost |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| **True HEPA H13 filter** | PM2.5 present in 73% of bad air days; catches both PM2.5 and PM10 | ₹800-1,500 |
| **Pre-filter (washable)** | Extends HEPA life in dusty NCR conditions | ₹200-400 |
| **Activated carbon layer** | O3 (3% of bad days) + traffic/cooking odors | ₹400-900 |

### Intelligence & Control
| Feature | Justification | BOM Cost |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| **PM2.5 sensor + auto mode** | Daily AQI swings of 34-42 points require automatic response | ₹700-1,600 |
| **High CADR fan (350+ m³/h)** | Winter average AQI of 161 demands rapid air circulation | ₹800-1,500 |
| **4-speed manual control** | User preference for noise vs performance | ₹200-400 |
| **Sleep mode + timer** | 81% of winter nights require overnight operation | ₹50-150 |

### User Experience
| Feature | Justification | BOM Cost |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| **Digital AQI display** | Real-time validation builds trust in polluted cities | ₹200-600 |
| **Filter change indicator** | Recurring revenue + user retention | Minimal |
| **Color-coded air quality** | Quick visual feedback (Good/Moderate/Poor/Severe) | Included in display |

### Differentiator
| Feature | Justification | BOM Cost |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| **Seasonal presets** | Winter (max power, 81% bad days), Summer (balanced, 41%), Monsoon (eco, 21%) | ₹50-150 |

**Total Core BOM:** ₹3,400-7,200

## Product Tiers

### Tier 1: Premium (NCR Launch)
**Target:** Delhi, Ghaziabad, Faridabad metro families  
**Features:** All must-haves + Wi-Fi app (₹400-900)  
**BOM:** ₹3,800-8,100  
**MRP:** ₹18,000-22,000  
**Positioning:** "Smart protection for India's most polluted cities"

### Tier 2: Essential (Year 2 Expansion)
**Target:** Bihar cities (Patna, Muzaffarpur), smaller NCR areas  
**Features:** All must-haves except Wi-Fi  
**BOM:** ₹3,400-7,200  
**MRP:** ₹12,000-15,000  
**Positioning:** "Proven filtration, essential features"

### Tier 3: Compact (Future)
**Target:** Single rooms, offices, budget-conscious  
**Features:** HEPA + pre-filter + manual 3-speed, no sensor  
**BOM:** ₹2,000-3,500  
**MRP:** ₹7,000-9,000  
**Positioning:** "Clean air, simplified"

---

## Pricing Strategy by Segment

### NCR Metro (Phase 1)
- **Premium tier only** (₹18-22K)
- Rationale: 51% bad air days, 22M population (Delhi), high purchasing power
- Winter premium pricing (Q1), monsoon pre-order discounts (Q3)

### NCR Extended + Bihar (Phase 2)
- **Premium + Essential tiers**
- Rationale: Similar pollution (40-49% bad days), lower income
- Essential tier captures price-sensitive buyers

### Corporate B2B
- **Bulk pricing:** 30% discount on Premium tier for 20+ units
- Target: NCR offices, schools, clinics

---

## Recurring Revenue
- **Replacement filters:** ₹2,500-3,500 retail (HEPA + carbon bundle)
- **Replacement cycle:** 6-8 months in NCR (higher PM load)
- **LTV estimate:** ₹6,000-7,000 over 3 years

---

## Success Metrics
- **Phase 1 (Year 1):** 5,000 units in NCR (0.02% penetration of 22M Delhi population)
- **Customer acquisition cost:** <₹2,000 (pre-winter digital campaigns)
- **Filter attachment rate:** >60% buy replacement within 8 months
